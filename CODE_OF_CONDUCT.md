# Code of Conduct

Compazio is an engineering community. Participation should make the project safer, clearer and easier to improve.

## Expected behavior

Contributors, maintainers and community members are expected to:

- be respectful and constructive, including during technical disagreement;
- critique code, designs and decisions rather than people;
- provide enough context for others to reproduce problems and review changes;
- respect privacy and avoid publishing credentials, private workspace data or personal information;
- accept maintainer decisions after reasonable technical discussion;
- avoid harassment, intimidation, discriminatory language, personal attacks and deliberate disruption.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, release communication and other project spaces where someone is representing Compazio.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions, reject contributions or temporarily/permanently restrict participation when behavior materially harms the project or its community.

For conduct reports that should not be public, contact `support@compazio.dev` and include `[CONDUCT]` in the subject. Reports will be reviewed as privately as practical.

Good-faith technical disagreement is welcome. Abuse is not.
